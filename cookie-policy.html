This policy explains how cookies and similar technologies are used on the IT Group website. It applies to every page of the site and should be read together with the cookie settings you choose in the banner.

01What are cookies

A cookie is a small text file that a website saves in your browser when you visit it. On your next visits the browser sends it back, so the site can remember your device and your choices — for example, your language or cookie settings — and work the way you expect.

  • First-party cookies are set by our website itself.
  • Third-party cookies are set by other services we use, such as analytics tools.
  • Session cookies are deleted when you close the browser, persistent cookies stay until they expire or you delete them.

Besides cookies, a site can store small amounts of data in your browser in other ways, such as local storage. In this policy we call all of these “cookies”.

02How we use cookies

We use cookies to keep the website working, to remember your cookie choice and — only if you agree — to understand how the site is used and to measure our advertising.

In line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the ePrivacy rules that apply in Latvia, we place necessary cookies without asking, because the site cannot work properly without them. All other cookies are set only after you give your consent in the cookie banner.

We never sell your data

Cookie data is used only for the purposes described on this page and is never sold to third parties.

03Cookie categories

Cookies on our website fall into three categories. You can switch analytics and marketing on or off separately.

Always on

Needed for the site to work, for example to remember your cookie choice. They don’t track you and can’t be switched off.

With your consent

Analytics

Show us which pages people visit and how they use the site, so we can improve it. The data is collected in aggregated form.

With your consent

Marketing

Help us measure our ad campaigns and show you relevant ads on other websites.

06Manage or withdraw consent

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time — it is as easy as giving it. Open the cookie settings, switch categories on or off and save. If you withdraw consent, we delete the related cookies and stop using them.

Your choice

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser. Keep in mind that some parts of the site may not work properly without necessary cookies.
